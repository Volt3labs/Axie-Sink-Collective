# Horizon 'Scrumble' Cup

The Horizon Scrumble Cup is an Axie Origins tournament where you don't enter with a finished team. It is organized by the "Axie Sink Collective".

You enter with **3 axie eggs**.

Two days before the tournament, the eggs will be hatch and we will cover the  live. Whatever comes out is what you have to work with.

From there, every player has to figure out the best possible build, runes, charms and strategy for their three new Axies.

## Core Rules

- Minimum **16 players** required for the Horizon Cup to run.
- Entry costs **3 Release Tickets** per player.
- Each player registers **3 Axie eggs**.
- The 3 eggs become the player's tournament roster.
- Eggs are hatched/revealed during the official **D-2 stream**.
- No swapping Axies after the reveal. No evolution.
- Players can use available **runes and charms** to create the best build possible from what they hatched.
- Standard Axie Origins competitive rules apply.
- Tournament organization and communication happen through **Discord**.

## Tournament Format

The exact format can change depending on the **number of registered players**.

The goal is to keep the group stage balanced and create a clean knockout bracket.

### Group Stage — Bo1

Players are divided into groups, ideally **4 players per group**.

Each player faces the other players in their group in **Best-of-1** matches.

Players are then ranked based on their group-stage results.

Depending on the total number of participants, the **best 8 or 16 players** advance to the knockout stage.

The exact number of groups, qualifying players and tiebreak rules will be announced once registration closes.

### Knockout Stage — Bo3

Once the group stage is finished, the tournament becomes direct elimination.

All knockout matches are **Best-of-3**.

Depending on the number of qualified players, the knockout stage can start with:

- Round of 16
- Quarter-finals
- Semi-finals
- Grand Final

Lose the series and you're out.

## Event Flow

The goal is to create a story around the tournament and progressively build attention toward the final stages.

### Main Platform

The main tournament broadcast will be hosted by @Gunlockeaxie on the platform he chose and I (@Volt3labs) will join him. Other streamers/host/content creators are welcome.

Discord remains the tournament HQ for players, match organization, results and announcements.

Clips and highlights can also be shared on **X / Axie Infinity Discord** to bring people toward the main stream.


### D-2 — Hatch & Reveal

This is the real start of the Horizon Cup.

The registered eggs are **hatched by players and we cover it on stream**.

Nobody knows exactly what team they are getting before the reveal.

Once the Axies are out, players have two days to study them, test builds and figure out how to make the best team possible with what they got.

The stream is also a chance to introduce the players, react to the best and weirdest hatches, and start building stories before the tournament. 

### D1 — Group Stage

All groups play their matches **in parallel**.

Streaming every match isn't necessary. The priority is keeping the tournament moving.

But we should still create content during the day:

- Jump into interesting matches
- Follow unexpected results
- Highlight strange or creative builds
- Talk with players
- Show standings
- Clip good moments

The goal is to create some hype and give viewers players and teams to follow during the knockout stage.

By the end of D1, the knockout bracket is set.

### D2 — Knockout Stage

The remaining players fight for the Horizon Cup.

The early knockout rounds are played in parallel.

They can be streamed or recorded, but full coverage isn't necessary.

From the **Semi-Finals onward, every match is streamed**.

**Semi-Final 1 → Semi-Final 2 → Grand Final**

At that point, viewers already know the players, their Axies and the builds they created from their original eggs.

## Discord Organization

Axie Sink Collective Discord is the tournament HQ.

It is used for:

- Registration information
- Announcements
- Player check-in
- Group assignments
- Match coordination
- Reporting results
- Standings and brackets

Players should be available on Discord while their stage of the tournament is running.

The idea is to keep organization lightweight and let players coordinate their group-stage matches directly whenever possible.

## Prize Pool

**300 AXS + Axie Sink Collective Prestige**

The exact AXS distribution between placements will be announced before the tournament.

Prestige will also be awarded based on tournament performance.

## The Idea

The Horizon Cup isn't about bringing the three most optimized Axies you already own.

Everyone starts with **three unknown eggs**.

You hatch them.

You see what fate gave you.

Then you have two days to turn that mess into the best team you can.

**Adapt. Build. Fight.**
