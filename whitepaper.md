# Axie Sink Collective Whitepaper

> Community-built initiatives to release more Axies and strengthen the Axie ecosystem.

---

# Vision

The Axie Sink Collective exists to increase the long-term sustainability of the Axie ecosystem by creating meaningful reasons to release Axies.

Rather than relying solely on official initiatives, the Collective empowers builders to launch independent projects that share a common release infrastructure. Every released Axie contributes to a single set of ecosystem metrics while each expedition remains free to innovate.

---

# Mission

Our mission is simple:

- Release as many Axies as possible.
- Create new utility for existing Axie NFTs.
- Build community-driven experiences.
- Strengthen the Axie ecosystem through open collaboration.

---

# Principles

- Community-first
- Open source whenever possible
- Independent from official development
- Transparent governance
- Shared infrastructure over fragmented projects

---

# The Collective

Anyone can propose ideas that benefit the Axie ecosystem.

If you believe an idea deserves to exist, discuss it with the community. Strong proposals can become official expeditions supported by the Collective.

The Collective is designed to make experimentation easy while ensuring successful projects contribute back to the ecosystem.

---

# Expeditions

Every initiative is called an **Expedition**.

Examples include:

- tournaments
- NFT collections
- games
- community events
- educational tools
- analytics
- creator initiatives

Each expedition should achieve at least one of the following:

- release Axies
- increase Axie utility
- strengthen the ecosystem

Projects begin as community discussions.

After refinement, the Council may approve them as official Horizon Fleet expeditions.

---

# Horizon Fleet

Horizon Fleet is the shared infrastructure connecting every expedition.

Although builders remain independent, every approved expedition that releases Axies must use the Horizon Fleet release contract.

This provides a common source of truth for:

- released Axies
- expedition statistics
- participant achievements
- Prestige
- ecosystem impact

Builders own their products.

The Collective owns the shared infrastructure.

---

# Prestige

Prestige measures contribution to the Collective.

Members earn Prestige through:

- participating in expeditions
- releasing Axies
- unlocking achievements
- holding expedition collections
- contributing to the community

Prestige is not transferable.

It represents reputation earned through participation.

---

# Governance

During the last week of each month, the ten members with the highest Prestige gain proposal rights.

Eligible members may submit proposals concerning:

- treasury allocation
- new expeditions
- ecosystem initiatives
- Collective improvements

Community discussion happens before voting.

The governance system is intended to evolve as the Collective grows.

---

# Treasury

The treasury funds future expeditions.

During the bootstrap phase, the founder contributes **10 AXS per day**.

Over time, funding is expected to come from:

- community donations
- grants
- sponsorships
- expedition revenue
- other sustainable sources

Treasury allocation is guided by governance.

---

# Founder Phase

The project begins with a single primary builder.

As the ecosystem grows:

- builders can lead expeditions
- contributors can join existing projects
- successful builders earn Prestige
- leadership becomes increasingly decentralized

---

# Open Collaboration

The Collective welcomes:

- developers
- artists
- event organizers
- creators
- researchers
- community leaders

Great ideas can come from anyone.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Create a sustainable ecosystem where independent community projects continuously generate value for Axie holders while permanently reducing Axie supply through meaningful releases.

The Axie Sink Collective is not one project.

It is a framework that allows hundreds of future projects to build together while sharing one common mission.

**Release more Axies. Build together.**
